Marble Storm – Privacy Policy

Marble Storm - Marble Shooter · Last updated: 25 November 2026

Marble Storm is a marble shooter puzzle game made by Avinash Kori, for players aged 13 and over. This policy explains what the app does with information about you.

What the app stores on your device

The following is saved only on your own device, so the game can show it again next time:

  • your level progress (unlocked levels, stars and best scores) and Endless mode records,
  • your coins, power-ups, unlocked skins and achievements,
  • your daily reward streak,
  • whether you have bought "Remove Ads" or another one-time item,
  • your settings: music, sound effect and voice volume, vibration, language (English or Hindi), difficulty and visual options.

This information is never sent to us. We do not operate any server and cannot see it. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data, deletes it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Android app shows ads provided by Google AdMob:

  • a banner on menu and level-select screens (never while you are playing a level),
  • a full-screen ad between levels from time to time (never during a level, and limited in how often it can appear), and
  • optional reward videos that you can choose to watch to continue a level, double your coins or get a free power-up. They are only shown when you ask for them.

When an ad is requested or shown, Google may collect and process:

Data Purpose
Advertising ID and app set ID (device identifiers for ads) Showing ads, measuring ad performance, frequency limits, and personalised ads where allowed
IP address and approximate location derived from it Choosing relevant ads and preventing fraud
Device and app information (model, OS version, app version) Showing ads that work on your device
Ad interactions (ads shown or tapped) and diagnostics Measuring ads and preventing fraud

This data is collected and processed by Google, not by us, and is shared with Google for advertising purposes. Learn more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Users in the EEA, UK and Switzerland: where the law requires it, the game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, and Settings shows "Privacy Settings" so you can change your choice at any time. You can play every part of the game without agreeing.

The web version of the game (played in a browser) shows no ads.

In-app purchases (Google Play)

You can optionally buy "Remove Ads", a Starter Pack or coin packs. Payments are handled entirely by Google Play: we never see or store your name, card or other payment details. The app receives only a confirmation from Google Play that a purchase was made, so it can give you the item and restore "Remove Ads" if you reinstall. Google's handling of payment data is described in the Google Payments Privacy Notice. "Remove Ads" removes banners and between-level ads; optional reward videos stay available if you want them.

Your choices

  • Advertising ID: on Android you can reset or delete it, or opt out of personalised ads, in Settings → Privacy → Ads (the exact path depends on your device).
  • Ads: reward videos are always optional. You can play every level without watching any video, and "Remove Ads" turns off the other ads.
  • Delete your data: uninstalling the game removes all of its data from your device. Clearing the app's storage in Android settings does the same. Purchases stay linked to your Google account by Google Play.

What the app does not do

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No analytics, tracking or crash reporting services of our own.
  • No location, contacts, camera, microphone or photo access.
  • No chat or sharing of anything you create.

Children

Marble Storm is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. Outside of the ads described above (provided by Google AdMob) and purchases handled by Google Play, the game itself does not collect personal information from anyone, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If a future version changes what data is handled, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the date above will change.
