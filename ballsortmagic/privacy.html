Privacy Policy

Ball Sort Magic - Color Game · Developer: Avinash Kori · Effective date: 5 November 2026

  • We have no accounts, no servers and no analytics. Your game progress stays on your phone.
  • The game shows ads from Google AdMob only when you choose to watch one for a booster. AdMob collects some device data to show and measure ads.
  • Uninstalling the game deletes all of its data from your device.

This policy explains what information the Android game Ball Sort Magic - Color Game (“the game”, “we”) uses, why, and the choices you have.

1. Information stored on your device

The game saves the following locally on your device so you can continue where you left off:

  • Level progress, stars, best moves and times, and daily puzzle results
  • Coins, unlocked themes, ball skins and tube styles
  • Settings such as sound, music, vibration and color symbols
  • A game in progress, so it can be resumed

This information is never sent to us. We do not operate any server and we cannot see it. Leaderboards in the game only rank results stored on your own device.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game offers optional rewarded ads: you can choose to watch a short ad to get an extra undo or shuffle. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. When an ad is requested or shown, Google may collect and process:

DataPurpose
Advertising ID (Android device identifier for ads)Showing ads, measuring ad performance, frequency limits, and personalised ads where allowed
IP address and approximate location derived from itChoosing relevant ads and preventing fraud
Device and app information (model, OS version, language, app version)Showing ads that work on your device
Ad interactions (ads shown, watched or tapped)Granting your reward, measuring ads, preventing fraud

This data is collected and processed by Google, not by us, and is shared with Google for advertising purposes. Learn more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Users in the EEA, UK and Switzerland: where the law requires it, the game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can still use every part of the game without agreeing.

3. Your choices

  • Ads are optional. No ad is ever needed to play or finish a level.
  • Advertising ID: on Android you can reset or delete it, or opt out of personalised ads, in Settings → Privacy → Ads (the exact path depends on your device).
  • Delete your data: uninstalling the game removes all game data from your device. Clearing the app’s storage in Android settings does the same.

4. Permissions

  • Internet / network access: needed only to load ads.
  • Advertising ID: used by Google AdMob as described above.

The game does not access your contacts, photos, camera, microphone, precise location or files.

5. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, contact us and we will help remove it.

6. Data security and retention

Game data stays on your device for as long as the game is installed. Data collected by Google AdMob is kept according to Google’s own retention policies.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
