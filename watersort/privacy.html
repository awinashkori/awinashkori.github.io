Water Sort – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

Water Sort is a puzzle game made by Avinash Kori. This policy explains what the app does with information about you.

What the app stores on your device

The following is saved only on your own device, so the game can show it again next time:

  • your level progress, stars, hints, daily streak and achievements,
  • your settings, such as language, sound, music, color-blind mode and the chosen theme.

This information is never sent to us. We do not operate any server and cannot see it. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data, deletes it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Android and iOS apps offer optional rewarded video ads, provided by Google AdMob: an ad plays only when you tap a button such as "Watch ad" to get a hint or extra time. There are no forced ads. When an ad is requested or shown, Google may collect and process:

Data Purpose
Advertising ID (device identifier for ads) Showing ads, measuring ad performance, frequency limits, and personalised ads where allowed
IP address and approximate location derived from it Choosing relevant ads and preventing fraud
Device and app information (model, OS version, app version) Showing ads that work on your device
Ad interactions (ads shown or tapped) Measuring ads and preventing fraud

This data is collected and processed by Google, not by us, and is shared with Google for advertising purposes. Learn more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Users in the EEA, UK and Switzerland: where the law requires it, the game asks for your consent before ads are requested, and Settings shows "Ad privacy options" so you can change your choice at any time. You can still use every part of the game without agreeing.

The web version of the game (played in a browser) shows no real ads.

Your choices

  • Advertising ID: on Android you can reset or delete it, or opt out of personalised ads, in Settings → Privacy → Ads (the exact path depends on your device). On iOS, use Settings → Privacy → Tracking.
  • Delete your data: uninstalling the game removes all of its data from your device. Clearing the app's storage in Android settings does the same.

What the app does not do

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No analytics, tracking or crash reporting services.
  • No location, contacts, camera or microphone access.
  • No in-app purchases.

Children

The game is designed for players aged 13 and over. Outside of the ads described above (provided by Google AdMob), the game itself does not collect personal information from anyone.

Changes

If a future version changes what data is handled, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the date above will change.
