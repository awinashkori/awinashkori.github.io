Block Shatter Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026 (optional rewarded ads added)

Block Shatter (Android package com.avinashkori.blockshatter) is a puzzle game made by Avinash Kori. This policy explains what the game does with your information. In short: your game progress stays on your device, and the only data that leaves it is what Google AdMob uses when you choose to watch an ad.

What the game stores

To keep your progress, the game saves the following only on your device:

  • your scores, best runs, levels, XP, missions, achievements and unlocked block styles
  • your settings (sound, voice, haptics, theme and controls)
  • a player name, chosen at random and changeable in Settings
  • a game in progress, so that you can continue it later

This data never leaves your device. We do not operate any server and we cannot see it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game has no ads you have to watch. It only offers optional "rewarded" videos: if you choose to watch one, you get a bonus, such as an extra hint or double XP. Ads never appear on their own and never interrupt play. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. When an ad is requested or shown, Google may collect and process:

  • Advertising ID (a device identifier for ads): showing and measuring ads, frequency limits, and personalised ads where allowed
  • IP address and the approximate location it implies: choosing relevant ads and preventing fraud
  • Device and app information (model, OS version, app version): showing ads that work on your device
  • Ad interactions (ads shown or tapped): measuring ads and preventing fraud

This data is collected and processed by Google, not by us, and is shared with Google for advertising purposes. Learn more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Users in the EEA, UK and Switzerland: where the law requires it, the game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy choices. Every part of the game works without agreeing.

What the game does not do

  • No account or sign-in
  • No analytics tools of our own
  • No in-app purchases
  • No access to your contacts, photos, location, camera or microphone

The game asks for vibration permission so that it can play haptic feedback, and for internet access only to load the optional ads. On Android you can reset or delete your Advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in Settings → Privacy → Ads (the exact path depends on your device).

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the game, or clearing its storage in your phone's settings, deletes everything it has saved. Data collected by Google AdMob is kept according to Google's own retention policies.

Children

The game is meant for players aged 13 and over. Outside of the optional ads described above (provided by Google AdMob), we do not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If a future update adds online features, such as worldwide leaderboards, this policy will be updated before that update is released, and the change will be described here.
