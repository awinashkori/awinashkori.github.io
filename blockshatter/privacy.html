Block Shatter Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

Block Shatter (Android package com.avinashkori.blockshatter) is a puzzle game made by Avinash Kori. This policy explains what the game does with your information. In short: the game does not collect, send or share any personal data.

What the game stores

To keep your progress, the game saves the following only on your device:

  • your scores, best runs, levels, XP, missions, achievements and unlocked block styles
  • your settings (sound, voice, haptics, theme and controls)
  • a player name, chosen at random and changeable in Settings
  • a game in progress, so that you can continue it later

This data never leaves your device. We do not have access to it.

What the game does not do

  • No account or sign-in
  • No advertising, and no advertising identifiers
  • No analytics or tracking tools
  • No in-app purchases
  • No access to your contacts, photos, location, camera or microphone

The game asks for vibration permission so that it can play haptic feedback.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the game, or clearing its storage in your phone's settings, deletes everything it has saved.

Children

The game does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If a future update adds online features, such as worldwide leaderboards, this policy will be updated before that update is released, and the change will be described here.
