SlideSnap – Privacy Policy

SlideSnap: Slide Puzzle 15 · Last updated: 14 November 2026

SlideSnap is a sliding number puzzle game made by Avinash Kori, suitable for children and adults. This policy explains what the app does with information about you.

What the app stores on your device

The following is saved only on your own device, so the game can show it again next time:

  • your level progress (stars and best moves for each level),
  • your Quick Play statistics (games played and won, best times and moves),
  • how many hints you have left, and an unfinished puzzle so you can continue it,
  • your settings: sound effects, music, vibration, light/dark mode, tile style and tile theme.

This information is never sent to us. We do not operate any server and cannot see it. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data, deletes it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Android app shows ads provided by Google AdMob:

  • a banner at the bottom of the home screen (never during a puzzle), and
  • an optional short video that you can choose to watch to get an extra hint. It is only shown when you ask for it.

Every ad request is marked as directed to children and limited to G-rated (suitable for all audiences) ads. This means Google does not show personalised (interest-based) ads in this app, and the app does not use your device's advertising ID. When an ad is requested or shown, Google may still collect and process limited information:

Data Purpose
IP address and approximate location derived from it Delivering ads and preventing fraud
Device and app information (model, OS version, app version) Showing ads that work on your device
Ad interactions (ads shown or tapped) Measuring ads and preventing fraud

This data is collected and processed by Google, not by us. Learn more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Because all ad requests are treated as coming from a child, the game does not ask for consent to personalised advertising. The web version of the game (played in a browser) shows no ads.

Your choices

  • Ads: the hint video is always optional. You can play every level without watching any video.
  • Delete your data: uninstalling the game removes all of its data from your device. Clearing the app's storage in Android settings does the same.

What the app does not do

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No analytics, tracking or crash reporting services.
  • No use of the advertising ID.
  • No location, contacts, camera, microphone or photo access.
  • No in-app purchases.

Children

SlideSnap is designed for all ages, including children under 13. The game itself does not collect personal information from anyone. The ads described above are requested with child-directed treatment as required by the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Google Play's Families policy.

Changes

If a future version changes what data is handled, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the date above will change.
