SlideSnap – Privacy Policy

SlideSnap - Slide Puzzle 15 · Last updated: 14 November 2026

SlideSnap is a sliding number puzzle game made by Avinash Kori, for players aged 13 and over. This policy explains what the app does with information about you.

What the app stores on your device

The following is saved only on your own device, so the game can show it again next time:

  • your level progress (stars and best moves for each level),
  • your Quick Play statistics (games played and won, best times and moves),
  • how many hints you have left, and an unfinished puzzle so you can continue it,
  • your settings: sound effects, music, vibration, light/dark mode, tile style and tile theme.

This information is never sent to us. We do not operate any server and cannot see it. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data, deletes it.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The Android app shows ads provided by Google AdMob:

  • a banner at the bottom of the home screen (never during a puzzle), and
  • an optional short video that you can choose to watch to get an extra hint. It is only shown when you ask for it.

When an ad is requested or shown, Google may collect and process:

Data Purpose
Advertising ID and app set ID (device identifiers for ads) Showing ads, measuring ad performance, frequency limits, and personalised ads where allowed
IP address and approximate location derived from it Choosing relevant ads and preventing fraud
Device and app information (model, OS version, app version) Showing ads that work on your device
Ad interactions (ads shown or tapped) and diagnostics Measuring ads and preventing fraud

This data is collected and processed by Google, not by us, and is shared with Google for advertising purposes. Learn more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy. Ads in the app are limited to content suitable for a general audience (PG).

Users in the EEA, UK and Switzerland: where the law requires it, the game asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown, and Settings shows "Ad privacy options" so you can change your choice at any time. You can play every part of the game without agreeing.

The web version of the game (played in a browser) shows no ads.

Your choices

  • Advertising ID: on Android you can reset or delete it, or opt out of personalised ads, in Settings → Privacy → Ads (the exact path depends on your device).
  • Ads: the hint video is always optional. You can play every level without watching any video.
  • Delete your data: uninstalling the game removes all of its data from your device. Clearing the app's storage in Android settings does the same.

What the app does not do

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No analytics, tracking or crash reporting services of our own.
  • No location, contacts, camera, microphone or photo access.
  • No in-app purchases.

Children

SlideSnap is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. Outside of the ads described above (provided by Google AdMob), the game itself does not collect personal information from anyone, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes

If a future version changes what data is handled, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the date above will change.
