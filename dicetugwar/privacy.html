Privacy Policy

Dice Tug War · Developer: Avinash Kori · Effective date: 7 November 2026

  • We have no accounts, no servers and no analytics. Everything stays on your phone.
  • No ads and no advertising identifiers.
  • Uninstalling the game deletes all of its data from your device.

This policy explains what information the Android game Dice Tug War (“the game”, “we”) uses, why, and the choices you have.

1. Information stored on your device

The game saves the following locally on your device so it can show it to you again:

  • Your match history (results, rounds played, best push)
  • Player names you type in for pass-and-play and multiplayer games
  • Settings such as the match length (Quick / Normal / Long)

This information is never sent to us. We do not operate any server and we cannot see it.

2. Advertising and analytics

The game shows no ads and uses no advertising or analytics services of any kind. It does not collect or transmit an advertising ID, usage statistics, or crash reports.

3. Your choices

  • Delete your data: uninstalling the game removes all of its data from your device. Clearing the app’s storage in Android settings does the same.

4. Permissions

The release version of the game requests no special permissions. It does not use the internet, and does not access your contacts, photos, camera, microphone, precise location or files.

5. Children

The game is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

6. Data security and retention

Game data stays on your device for as long as the game is installed.

7. Changes to this policy

If a future version of the game adds features that handle data (for example online play), this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the date above will change.

8. Contact
