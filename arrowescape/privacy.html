Privacy Policy

Arrow Escape - Neon Maze (Android package com.avinashkori.arrowescape)
Last updated: 9 November 2026

This policy explains what information the game Arrow Escape - Neon Maze ("the app") uses and why. The app is made by an independent developer ("we").

1. Information we collect ourselves

None. The app has no accounts, no sign-in and no servers of its own. Your game progress (levels, stars, hints, streak, album, settings such as theme, language, sound and vibration) is saved only on your device. It is never sent to us. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app can show rewarded video ads, only when you choose to watch one (for example to get a free hint). Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and process:

  • your device's advertising ID (Android Advertising ID);
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it;
  • device and app information (model, operating system, language, app version);
  • interactions with ads (views, taps) and diagnostic data such as crash logs from the ads SDK.

This data is handled by Google under its own policies: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Your choices. In regions where consent is required (such as the EEA, UK and Switzerland) the app shows Google's consent form before any ads are requested, and you can change your choice at any time from Settings → Ad privacy choices. On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your device settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads).

3. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ads are shown only when the player chooses to watch them.

4. Permissions

The app uses internet / network-state access and the advertising-ID permission only for the ads described above. It does not access your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or location.

5. Data security and retention

We do not store any of your data on our side. Data processed by Google AdMob is kept according to Google's policies.

6. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new "Last updated" date.
